## Dynamic chapter navigation correction

The formal reader no longer presents a permanent or predefined chapter structure. Navigation is derived only from non-empty level 1–3 headings that exist in the current document body:

- zero or one authored heading: no chapter control is rendered;
- two or more authored headings: one on-demand `章節` control opens a drawer containing only those headings;
- selecting a heading closes the drawer, updates the document hash and moves focus to the matching heading;
- no fixed `目的／範圍／流程／內控／ISO` headings are generated by the reader, and the historical 14-chapter HTML prototype remains evidence only.
